Before we begin

Doctor Bernd, your professor, will present a pediatric patient and question you on the differential diagnosis, the medical tests you would recommend and the treatment. His questions adapt to your answers.

  • This is a spoken examination. Doctor Bernd speaks his questions aloud. Answer by voice (your microphone turns on by itself after he finishes speaking) or by typing. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari, with headphones if you can.
  • Ask for the history, examination and test results you need. Nothing is handed to you.
  • Press Hold to pause the conversation at any time, and Continuation to pick up where you left off. A held session is kept in this browser, even if you close the page.
  • Say or type stop (on its own) or press Stop and review to end. Doctor Bernd will then review every quiz question with you, evaluate your performance and recommend reading.
Voice answers are not available in this browser, so you will type your answers. Doctor Bernd can still speak if your browser supports it. For full voice, open this page in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari.
